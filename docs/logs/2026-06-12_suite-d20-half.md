## Observations (d20 scale, fixed sane recipe)

1. **At d20 scale, the measured interpretability cost is small overall in this
   partial suite**: both constraints together cost 0.65 pts overall
   (0.8470→0.8405). The cost concentrates in cat0 (−3.0 pts, ~17% relative) and
   cat1 (−2.7 pts, ~11% rel); cat2/3 ≈ flat; cats 4-5 barely move. This is far
   milder than the thesis-era "5-10% (bn) to ~25% (both) in cats 0-3" —
   **hypothesis: part of the thesis-reported interpretability cost was
   under-training under the legacy schedule**, exactly what the d152 half + the
   legacy comparability runs will adjudicate. Do not generalize this to thesis
   scale until those runs land.
2. **Constraints appear to reduce seed variance in this 3-seed d20 slice** (none
   ±0.0028 vs both ±0.0009 overall; same pattern per-category) — consistent with
   a regularization effect, but still a small-n observation.
3. d20 models nearly match the 677k thesis model on the easy categories (0.951/
   0.905 vs 0.959/0.927) — the capacity gap lives entirely in cats 0-3.
